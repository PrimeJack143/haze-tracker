About Haze Tracker - Dangcagan

Haze Tracker is a real-time air quality monitoring platform dedicated to Dangcagan, Bukidnon, Philippines.

Our mission is simple: to help residents stay informed about the air they breathe.

Using live data from OpenWeatherMap (OWM), the system tracks key air pollutants including PM2.5, PM10, Carbon Monoxide (CO), Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2), Ozone (O3), and Sulfur Dioxide (SO2), and converts them into an easy-to-understand Air Quality Index (AQI) from 1 (Good) to 5 (Very Poor).

Features:
- Live AQI status for Dangcagan
- Detailed breakdown of 6 major pollutants
- 7-Day AQI trend history
- Auto-updates every 30 minutes
- Clean, mobile-friendly design

Whether you're planning outdoor activities, concerned about haze, or just want to check your local environment, Haze Tracker gives you accurate and timely updates.
